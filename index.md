I am a Member of Technical Staff at [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/). Previously, I was a Distinguished Research Scientist at [Meta AI](http://research.facebook.com/ai), an Assistant Professor (with tenure) at [Delft University of Technology](http://www.tudelft.nl), a post-doctoral researcher at [UC San Diego](http://www.ucsd.edu), and a a Ph.D. student at [Tilburg University](http://www.tilburguniversity.edu).

I am interested in a variety of topics in artificial intelligence. In particular, I am interested in embedding models, large-scale weakly supervised learning, reasoning, cost-sensitive learning, and language modeling.
